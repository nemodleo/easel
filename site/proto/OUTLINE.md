# easel.d-t-it.com rebuilt under one rule

Every screen has one thing to look at and one thing to do. The first screen's one thing is Easel's core act, large. A "screen" is one viewport tall on a desktop. On a phone it can be longer, but it keeps the same single focus.

| # | Screen | Its one question | One thing to look at | One thing to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The screen and its file** (prototype 2) | What is it? | A real board beside its real HTML; your retype rewrites one line | Retype the title (then Copy the command in the bar) |
| 2 | **Ask an agent** | Does an agent really work in it? | The real recording at content width, opening on the request and the board turning into the ticket (prototype 1's player) | Play / jump to a moment (Ask, Undo) |
| 3 | **The whole run is one undo** | Can I trust it with my files? | One still: "Run undone · 1 board restored" | Watch it at 0:28 (seeks screen 2) |
| 4 | **Many agents, no overwrites** | What if two agents edit the same board? | The fork screenshot, large, with its honest "scripted" line | None (read) |
| 5 | **Inside the apps you already use** | Do I have to switch windows? | Two parallel columns, Claude desktop app and Codex, each with its steps and one piece of evidence (the transcript, the config `connect` writes) | Copy `npx easel-canvas connect` |
| 6 | **Share a view-only copy** | How do I show it to someone? | The live export, whole and composed (prototype 3), with "Play the app" | Play the app |
| 7 | **Side by side** | Why not my design tool or a chat artifact? | The three-column table, alone on the screen, rows as large as the headings today | None (read) |
| 8 | **Start** | Can I try it this minute? | The two commands | Copy (the orange); GitHub, npm, Docs under them |

## Merged or cut from today's page

- **The first screen's card over the canvas: cut.** The canvas moves to screen 6, where it proves Share, the thing it actually is.
- **"Watch, then run": becomes screen 2**, at content width, with no heading row competing with it.
- **"Why now": cut as a section.** Its one sentence ("The agents moved into the repo. The design didn't.") becomes the line above screen 1's lab, or the page's description. Three numbered columns are a list, which the rule rejects.
- **Four proofs: become screens 1 to 4.** "Edit in place" is screen 1 itself. "Agents as teammates" splits into screens 2 and 3. "Many agents" is screen 4. "Share" is screen 6. No more small stills in a grid.
- **How it works + Start: merged into screen 8.** Both said "run this command". The three-step file → canvas → agent line becomes one line of fact under the commands. `connect` lives in screen 5, where it is needed.
- **Inside Claude and Codex: screen 5**, after the agent proofs. It answers the next doubt (another window?) and carries its own command.
- **Comparison table: screen 7**, near the end and full size. It reads as a conclusion once the mechanism has been shown, not as a claim before it.
- **The orange.** One per screen: Copy in the bar on screen 1; Play on screen 6; Copy on screen 8. Screens 2 to 5 and 7 have none, or the recording's own Play.
